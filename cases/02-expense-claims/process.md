# Case 02: Employee travel expense claims

## Process text (paste this into the tool)

Nordbakk AS employees claim travel expenses after a trip.

1. Before travel, the employee gets the trip approved by their manager.
2. After the trip, the employee submits one claim per trip with itemised receipts and a business purpose for each expense.
3. Finance checks that each receipt is itemised and shows proof of payment, and that receipt amounts match the claim lines.
4. Finance checks each line against the travel policy: allowed categories, per-diem rates, and that meal claims list attendees.
5. Claims with unclear or out-of-policy items go back to the employee with a question. The employee's explanation is judged case by case.
6. The employee's manager approves the claim, confirming the expenses are reasonable and business-related.
7. Claims from senior leaders are approved by the next level up, not by someone reporting to them.
8. Approved claims are paid in the next payroll or payment run.
9. A sample of paid claims is audited later. Amounts paid against policy may be demanded back.
