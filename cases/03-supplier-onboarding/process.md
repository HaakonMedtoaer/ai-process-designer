# Case 03: New supplier onboarding and bank detail changes

## Process text (paste this into the tool)

Nordbakk AS sets up new suppliers in the supplier register before they can be paid.

1. A buyer submits a request to add a new supplier, with the supplier's legal name, org number and contact.
2. The supplier sends its registration documents and bank account details.
3. AP checks that the legal name and org number match the public business register, and that the bank account holder matches the legal entity.
4. AP screens the company and its owners against sanctions lists.
5. AP phones the supplier to confirm the bank details, using a number found independently, not the one in the email or form.
6. If something doesn't line up (name mismatch, recently changed bank account, odd ownership), AP investigates before going further.
7. A second person, who did not enter the data, approves and activates the supplier record. Payments stay blocked until then.
8. Later requests to change a supplier's bank account go through steps 5 to 7 again, because fake change requests by email are a known fraud route.
9. Each check is logged with who did it, when and against which source.
