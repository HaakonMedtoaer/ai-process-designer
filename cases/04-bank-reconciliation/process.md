# Case 04: Month-end bank reconciliation

## Process text (paste this into the tool)

Nordbakk AS reconciles each bank account against the general ledger every month.

1. At the start of the month, the accountant downloads the previous month's bank statement.
2. Bank transactions are matched to ledger entries using matching rules (amount, date, reference).
3. The accountant matches the remaining items by hand, looking at descriptions, invoices and payment files.
4. Remaining differences are classified: outstanding payments, deposits in transit, timing differences, or errors.
5. For errors and unexplained items, the accountant works out the cause, which can mean asking other departments or the bank.
6. The accountant proposes correcting entries. Someone else posts them; the preparer does not post their own corrections.
7. Items that stay open past the policy limit are escalated to the controller.
8. The controller reviews and signs the reconciliation by the monthly deadline.
